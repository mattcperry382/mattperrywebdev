BYU Office of Research Computing

2021-2024 Provo, UT

While I worked at the BYU Office of Research Computing, I had the opportunity to redesign the entire website. I was in charge of modernizing the website to have a more clear and readable UI. Areas of major rework and improvement include large cards for a broad navigation design across the website.

Other accomplishments while at the BYU Office of Research Computing include:

  • Automated Mattermost API calls, significantly increasing ticketing and account approval response times
  • Created first publicly-available Wireguard VPN script for PHP utilizing MVC UI design to manage VPN clients
  • Redesigned UI to allow viewing resources used by and allocated to the supercomputer
  • Used MySQL shell, Unix CLI, Git, Docker regularly to operate database, install dependencies, and manage version control
