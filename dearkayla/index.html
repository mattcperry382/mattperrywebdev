Dear Kayla


I just wanted to take a minute to say I’m sorry. I know I didn’t get to the laundry, the dishes, or even fill the water in the humidifier like I said I would. Those things might seem small, but I know they matter to you, and they should matter to me too. You do so much every single day to keep our home running and to take care of us, and I should’ve done my part without needing a reminder.

Sometimes I get caught up in trying to do everything I can to provide for our family and keep us moving forward, and I forget that part of that is helping with the everyday things that make our life comfortable. I don’t ever want you to feel like you’re doing this alone, because we’re a team. I want to be better at showing that through my actions, not just my words.

I love you so much. You mean more to me than anything else in this world. I’m so grateful for your patience and your kindness when I fall short. You’ve always believed in me, even when I’m tired or distracted or buried in work, and that means everything. I see how hard you work, and I want to match that effort in every way I can.

Thank you for loving me, for trusting me, and for being by my side while I figure all this out. I’m trying hard to be the man you deserve, the kind who shows love not just by saying it, but by doing the dishes, folding the laundry, and filling up that humidifier without being asked.

I love you more than words can say, and I’m really lucky to have you.

❤️ Matt
